Method

Starting January 2026, each completed month keeps exactly three concepts. A concept must be more than a launch or a loud phrase: it should introduce a new way to frame a problem, change what can be built or measured, and generate work that follows.

Every entry has three links: Origin for the first articulation, Proof for an implementation or result, and Context for the best route into the idea.

Older breakthroughs are not backfilled into this archive. The current month is provisional and will be frozen after the month closes.
